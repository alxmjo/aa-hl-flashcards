**New cards**

- **Exponential equations:** solving equations such as 3 × 2^x = 75 and 5^x = 2^(x+3) with logarithms.
- **Logarithmic functions:** y = log_a x as the inverse of y = a^x; the domain and vertical asymptote of y = p log_a(x − h) + k; finding the inverse of a logarithmic or exponential function.
