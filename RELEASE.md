v2026-11-19: Bearings cards in 1.7
Two changes to the bearings cards in **1.7 Right Angled Triangle Trigonometry**.

**Changed**

- **1.7-E02** (the bearing of A from B): the rule is now a single step. Add 180°, then subtract 360° if the result is 360° or more. The Extra field also gives the equivalent form θ ± 180°, whichever gives a bearing from 000° to 359°. Your review progress on this card is kept.

**Retired**

- **1.7-E03** (finding a bearing from north–south and east–west displacements). To remove it from your collection after importing, open **Browse**, search for:

  `ID:1.7-E03`

  then select the card and delete it.

**How to update**

Download the deck from the usual link and import it with **Import any deck presets** turned on and the other import options at their defaults. Your review progress is kept.
